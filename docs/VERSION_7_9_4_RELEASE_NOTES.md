# Version 7.9.4

## Conservative Manager Placement Thresholds

Manager-level RTS placement recommendations are now intentionally harder to qualify.

### Worth Evaluating
A candidate must generally meet all three:
- 25+ net-new covered stores
- 15%+ of the manager's total store footprint improved
- 20%+ of the manager's current gaps addressed

### Strong
A candidate must generally meet all three:
- 35+ net-new covered stores
- 20%+ of the manager's total store footprint improved
- 25%+ of the manager's current gaps addressed

Candidates below those thresholds remain available through gap and simulation tools,
but are not surfaced as staffing recommendations.

This keeps the placement engine focused on material, concentrated opportunities rather
than mathematically positive but difficult-to-justify headcount additions.
