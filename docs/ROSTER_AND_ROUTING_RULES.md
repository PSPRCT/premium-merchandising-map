# Roster and Routing Rules

## Universal roster rule

All rows on the applicable RTS roster count in strategic planning. Temporary
vacation, PTO, LOA, or an `ActiveForRouting = False` source value does not remove
the RTS from the planned network. Permanent removals are handled by deleting the
person from the source roster.

## Premium Merchandising

Eligible RTS pool: every RTS on the Premium Merchandising roster.

## One Walmart core

Eligible RTS pool: every RTS in `RCT_RTS (25).csv`.

## Dedicated teams

P&G, Unilever, and Tyson eligible pool: every RTS in `RCT_RTS (24).csv`.

## Distance

All programs and teams use a 75-mile radius.
